SEC-bench Pro expands the SEC-bench benchmark family toward harder, project-specific security evaluations. The current leaderboard release focuses on Chromium V8 and reports official results for the `source_files` setup.

## Citation

### Benchmark Family

SEC-bench Pro is part of the SEC-bench benchmark family. Until a dedicated SEC-bench Pro paper is released, please cite the SEC-bench paper for the benchmark family and link the SEC-bench Pro repository when you reference the V8 release or use the public harness.
